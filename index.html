Overview

Architecture of
Convergence

Traditional unmanned systems force compromise. Phantom eliminates this through a single Persistent Core that accepts any mission configuration through the QRAM interface system.

01
Persistent Core
One standardized fuselage carries flight control, power management, AI computing, and communications. Mass-producible. Field-repairable. 10-year service life.
02
QRAM Interface
Quick-Release Aerodynamic Mount provides mechanical, electrical, and data connectivity between core and mission modules. No tools. No calibration. Three minutes.
03
Solar Persistence
480W peak solar harvest enables unlimited daylight endurance. 222Wh battery bridges nighttime operations. The wing is the energy system.
04
Thrust Vectoring
Single-engine gimbal propulsion with 0–90° pitch and ±25° yaw enables vertical launch, hover, and fixed-wing cruise from one propulsion unit.
05
Mercury AI
Real-time 3D reconstruction, object detection, swarm coordination, and predictive modeling — running at the edge on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.
06
Global Deployment
Six wing configurations cover Arabian Desert, Maritime, Alpine, Tropical, High-Speed, and VTOL operations. One platform. Every environment.
