ARCHITECTURE
01 — Overview

Architecture of convergence.

Traditional unmanned systems force compromise between endurance, payload and deployment. Phantom dissolves that trade-off through a modular architecture — one persistent core, infinite mission skins.

/ 01

Persistent Core

One standardised fuselage carries flight control, power management, AI compute and comms across every mission profile.

/ 02

Solar-Electric VTOL

Zero runway required. Vertical lift retracts into a low-drag cruise frame for maximum aerodynamic glide.

/ 03

Solar Integration

High-efficiency solar wing skin yields up to 40% less energy drain — extending zero-carbon range over vast distances.
