01 — Built for the Sky

Compute born for flight.

Mercury runs on a custom aerospace-grade compute module, designed in-house for the thermal, vibration and power constraints of sustained autonomous flight — not adapted from ground hardware. Sealed, vibration-isolated, and rated for continuous operation across the full Phantom flight envelope.

04 — Anticipation

From observation to anticipation.

Not just data — recommendations. Mercury models fire spread, pollution transport and infrastructure degradation, delivering time-stamped action guidance while humans keep final authority.

Wildfire

Spread Modeling

Coupled atmosphere-fire model. Inputs: fire perimeter, fuel type, terrain slope, wind field. Output: spread probability map, time-of-arrival contours, firebreak placement.

Emissions

Plume Transport

Gaussian plume model with AURA-measured wind profiles. Source location from detection, emission rate from concentration, downwind impact for evacuation planning.

Infrastructure

Degradation Forecast

Trend analysis from repeat surveys. Erosion rate from 3D change detection, pipeline exposure trajectory, failure-probability timeline for predictive maintenance.
